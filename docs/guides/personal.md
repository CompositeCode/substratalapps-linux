# Your apps, inbox and account
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## Home: your apps

[Home]({{ '/screens/app-01/' | relative_url }}) shows every app you can use and its access status. Select an app for [its details]({{ '/screens/app-02/' | relative_url }}); **Open** launches it with a short-lived app token, so you don't sign in again. Apps you no longer have are under [Past apps]({{ '/screens/app-05/' | relative_url }}).

## Inbox

The [Inbox]({{ '/screens/ntf-01/' | relative_url }}) holds every notification sent to you by any app, plus Substratal's security and account alerts, for 90 days. It's always complete, even when a desktop notification didn't appear.

**On Linux and Windows**, notifications arrive while the app is running: it checks for new ones every minute and shows a desktop notification. There's no push service for Linux desktops, so if the app is closed you'll see them next time you open it. On iOS, macOS, Android and the Web they're pushed.
{: .note }

## Organizations

[Organizations]({{ '/screens/org-01/' | relative_url }}) are teams. Organization admins invite members, grant apps to the whole organization or to chosen members, and can pay for Standard for the members they choose.

## Plan & Billing

Everyone starts on **Free**. **Standard** is $6 per person per month and unlocks every standard app. You buy it for yourself in [Plan & Billing]({{ '/screens/bil-01/' | relative_url }}), or an organization buys it for you. Payment always happens on Stripe's page in your browser; when you come back, the Hub waits a few seconds for the change to arrive and then shows your new plan.

## Account and security

[Account]({{ '/screens/acc-01/' | relative_url }}) has your profile. [Security]({{ '/screens/acc-04/' | relative_url }}) has your password, two-factor authentication and the list of places you're signed in, where you can sign other sessions out. [Privacy]({{ '/screens/acc-07/' | relative_url }}) lets you export your data or delete your account.

## Settings and themes

[Settings]({{ '/screens/set-01/' | relative_url }}) holds your language, time zone, notifications and appearance. Pick **Match system**, **Light** or **Dark**, and one of five light and five dark themes. The defaults are **Moss Paper** (light) and **Forest Floor** (dark). If your desktop uses high contrast, the Hub follows it instead of the theme.
